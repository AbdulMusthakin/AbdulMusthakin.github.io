Who I Am

I'm a first-year student at the University of Warwick on the four-year MMath Mathematics course.

Most of my time goes to self-directed pure mathematics. My taste runs structural and categorical: I'd rather understand why a construction is the universal one than compute with it, and I like an intuition-first overview before the formal machinery.

Alongside that I'm interested in the mathematical foundations of modern physics, and in quantitative finance as a possible direction after university.
