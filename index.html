§1

Introduction

I am a first-year student at the University of Warwick, studying mathematics on the four-year MMath course.

Most of my time is spent on independent study of mathematics. Thus far, my studies have included topics in real and complex analysis, differential geometry, and algebraic topology.

Remark 1.1. I prefer to have the intuition behind difficult ideas before I learn the underlying theory.

Remark 1.2. Beyond pure mathematics, I am interested in the mathematical foundations of physics. I am also considering quantitative finance as a possible pathway following university.
